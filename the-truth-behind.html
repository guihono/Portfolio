The game

The Truth Behind is an action RPG about Glasya, a demon who works for the Devil as an angel assassin. She is sent through history to hunt archangels, and every time she kills one she absorbs part of its power. That power starts to change how she sees angels, humans and herself. And the truth is not what she was told.

My role

I was responsible for the narrative design and the game design in this game's document, which has 27 pages: the narrative, the systems, the enemies and the bosses. The narrative has a prelude, ten chapters and a final chapter in Hell. Each battle takes place in a real city at a real moment in history, from Jericho in 9000 BC to the Vatican in 1928.

I researched the archangels and the history behind every battle, and I used it to design 13 boss fights, each with its own weapon and abilities. I also designed the combat and level system, Glasya's skills and the skill loadout. I have started building the game in Unreal Engine, and I am learning the engine while doing it.
