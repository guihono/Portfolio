00 — About

I build games that tell stories worth playing.

Hi — I'm Guilherme, a storywriter, game programmer, and game designer from Brazil. I've been writing stories and playing games since childhood; two years ago I started building them.

I'm pursuing my Bachelor's in Game Design and Development at Rochester Institute of Technology (expected 2027), working across Unreal Engine blueprints, Unity C#, GameMaker, and full narrative systems design.

Beyond code and engines, I care deeply about the stories games tell — and I'm always pushing to make them unforgettable. I take on challenges not just to learn, but to grow into the developer those games deserve.

I also build websites. You're on one. ;)

No Matter What

My role · Writer · Narrative Designer · Twine Developer

A branching superhero story inspired by Invincible and superhero comics, where your choices decide whether you become a hero, a vigilante, or a villain.

What I did

Story & Systems Design

  • Created the original characters and the story
  • Designed the Choice Continuum, a protector-vs-punisher meter that drives status, reputation, and endings
  • Planned a five-issue branching arc where every choice has a cost
  • Weighted choices so big decisions leave marks that never go away
  • Revised the story from professor feedback

Writing & Implementation

  • Prototyped scenes as screenplays in Trelby before adapting them to interactive prose
  • Built it in Twine with SugarCube
  • Page theme and status chip react to how society sees the player
  • "X will remember that" callbacks for major choices
  • Comic-style "Issues" and choices laid out around the text

Status: In development — aiming for a more complete build by the end of the year.
