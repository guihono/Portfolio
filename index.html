Hi — I'm Guilherme, a storywriter, game programmer, and game designer from Brazil. I've been writing stories and playing games since childhood; two years ago I started building them.

I'm pursuing my Bachelor's in Game Design and Development at Rochester Institute of Technology (expected 2027), working across Unreal Engine blueprints, Unity C#, GameMaker, and full narrative systems design.

Beyond code and engines, I care deeply about the stories games tell — and I'm always pushing to make them unforgettable. I take on challenges not just to learn, but to grow into the developer those games deserve.

I also build websites. You're on one. ;)

Nature of the Unknown

A 2D action-platformer set in a dark, atmospheric Amazon rainforest. Players control a grasshopper protagonist navigating a dangerous world of hostile insects and creatures, mastering fast movement, melee combat, and a timing-based dodge-parry system to survive increasingly difficult encounters and boss fights.

The visual direction is entirely original — pixel-art characters, enemies, environments, UI, and effects all hand-crafted to reflect the mystery and danger of the rainforest setting.

Programming

  • Player movement, jumping & double jump
  • Melee combat system
  • Dash / dodge-parry & counterattacks
  • Projectile reflection mechanic
  • Health, damage & game state systems
  • Enemy AI & behaviors
  • Multi-phase spider boss

Design & Art

  • Game & combat design
  • Level design & enemy placement
  • Tutorial & progression systems
  • Difficulty balancing
  • Original pixel art & animation
  • UI design & VFX
  • Playtesting & debugging
