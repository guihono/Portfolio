No Matter What

Narrative designer · Twine developer

A branching superhero narrative inspired by Invincible and superhero comics.

The game

In No Matter What you gain powers, and the choices you make decide whether you become a hero, a vigilante or a villain. The narrative is told in five issues, like a comic, and every choice has a cost. Big decisions leave marks, and the game remembers them.

My role

I created the original characters and the plot, and I was responsible for the narrative design and the Twine development. The game is built in Twine using the SugarCube format.

I designed the Choice Continuum, a meter that goes from protector to punisher and drives your status, your reputation and the endings. I draft every scene as a screenplay in Trelby first and then adapt it into interactive text. I also made the page theme and the status chip react to how the city sees you, and I added "X will remember that" callbacks for major choices. I have also revised the narrative based on feedback from my professor.

The game is still in development, and I am aiming for a more complete build by the end of the year.
