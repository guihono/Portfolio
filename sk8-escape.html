Sk8 Escape

Gameplay programmer · UI designer

An endless runner made in C# with Visual Studio 2022. A skater runs from the cops and has to manage their speed.

This was a class project with a team of 5, so I built my parts to fit with what everybody else was making.

I programmed the character movement, which is the most important part of a runner because it decides how the game feels. I made the Start, Pause and Game Over screens. I also made the afterimage effect, the trail behind the skater that shows how fast they are going.

What I did

  • Programmed the character movement
  • Designed the Start, Pause and Game Over screens
  • Made the afterimage effect that shows speed
  • Worked with a team of 5 and built my parts to fit theirs
