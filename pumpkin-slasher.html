Pumpkin Slasher Bloodbath

Narrative designer · Level designer · Environment artist

A retro horror hack-and-slash made in GameMaker for a game jam.

The game

Pumpkin Slasher Bloodbath was made for a game jam with the theme "Nostalgia". You play as a kid who is playing a horror game when glitches start to appear. You have to fix them before the monster gets out into the real world.

My role

I made this game with a team of 3 during the game jam. I was responsible for the narrative, writing the story of the kid and the glitches, and I also helped shape the overall concept of the game.

I designed the levels and built the maps, and I created the environment sprites, like the houses, the lakes and the pumpkin patches. Our game won the Creativity Award of the jam.

In short

  • Wrote the story
  • Helped shape the overall concept
  • Designed the levels and built the maps
  • Created the environment sprites: houses, lakes and pumpkin patches
  • Won the Creativity Award with my team
