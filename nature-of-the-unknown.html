Nature of the Unknown

Solo developer · Programmer · Designer · Pixel artist

A 2D action platformer set in a dark Amazon rainforest.

The game

Nature of the Unknown is a 2D action platformer set in a dark Amazon rainforest. You play as a grasshopper who has to fight insects and bosses, and timing is everything. If you dodge at the right moment you parry, counterattack, and even reflect projectiles back at the enemy.

My role

I am developing this game by myself, so I am responsible for everything in it. On the programming side I built the player movement with jump and double jump, melee combat, the dash and dodge-parry with counterattacks, projectile reflection, health and damage, the enemy behaviors, and a spider boss with multiple phases.

On the design side I was responsible for the combat design, the level design, the encounters and enemy placement, the tutorials, the progression, the game states and the difficulty balancing. I also made all of the pixel art, animations, UI and visual effects, and I handle the playtesting and debugging. The game is still in development, and I am also designing its narrative.

In short

  • Gameplay programming: movement, melee, dodge-parry, counterattacks, projectile reflection
  • Enemy behaviors and a multi-phase spider boss
  • Combat design and level design
  • Encounters, enemy placement, tutorials and progression
  • Difficulty balancing
  • Pixel art, animations, UI and visual effects
  • Playtesting, debugging and production
