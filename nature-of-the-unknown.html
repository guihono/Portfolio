Nature of the Unknown

Solo developer · Programmer · Designer · Pixel artist

A 2D action platformer in Unity, set in a dark Amazon rainforest. You play a grasshopper who fights insects and bosses by timing dodges.

I am making this game by myself, so I do everything. The whole game is built around one idea: the dodge-parry. If you time your dodge right, you can counterattack, and you can even send projectiles back at the enemy.

On the code side I wrote the movement, the jumps, the melee attack, the dodge, the health and damage, the enemies and a spider boss with more than one phase. On the design side I decided how the fights are set up, where the enemies go, how the game teaches you the controls and how hard it gets. I also drew all the pixel art and the animations.

The video is the game as it is right now. It is still in progress, and I am writing the story for it too.

What I did

  • Player movement, jump and double jump
  • Melee combat
  • Dash and dodge-parry, with counterattacks
  • Reflecting projectiles back at enemies
  • Health, damage and game states
  • Behavior for each kind of enemy
  • A spider boss with more than one phase
  • Designed the game around the dodge-parry
  • Encounter design and enemy placement
  • Tutorials, progression and difficulty balancing
  • All the pixel art and animation, drawn from scratch
  • UI and visual effects
  • Playtesting, fixing bugs and running the project
